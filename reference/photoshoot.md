# Kikiewa — Photoshoot Shot List

**For the photographer and for the website.** Every shot has a filename. Save each final photo with exactly that name into the `photoshoot/` folder in the website project, then run `npm run photos:import`. The site places it automatically, and anything not yet shot keeps its placeholder.

Total: **43 core shots** (+ 4 optional phone-crop versions of the hero shots, + products and team when known). Plan a full day: morning at Ebute, afternoon at Nitel.

---

## Technical brief

- **Format:** JPEG, sRGB, full resolution straight from the camera (at least 3000px on the long edge). No Instagram exports, no WhatsApp-compressed files.
- **Orientation:** as listed per shot. **L** = landscape (3:2), **P** = portrait (4:5), **S** = square-friendly (shoot P with room around the subject).
- **Light:** soft, warm, natural where possible. Avoid harsh overhead fluorescent light. Same white balance across the shoot so the site feels like one story.
- **Look:** calm, clean, premium. Tidy every surface before each shot. Hide cables, product clutter, phones and price lists.
- **Brand colours on set:** plum, magenta and soft pink accents (towels, robes, flowers, props) on cream and neutral backgrounds. Avoid strong competing colours (bright green, orange, red).
- **Retouching:** natural only. Clean up blemishes from the set, not from skin texture. **Never** alter skin results on treatment shots.
- **Leave space:** for hero shots, keep the subject slightly off-centre with clear space for text.
- **Consent:** every recognisable person signs a photo release before the shoot (staff, models and clients). Use the "Hands and detail" alternatives when someone prefers not to be identified. **No before/after photos without a separate written consent.**
- **Aesthetics:** no needles, syringes or injections in any frame. Show consultation, skin analysis, devices, products and the clean room.
